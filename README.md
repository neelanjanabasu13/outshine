# Trade Grade

I want to build FixFirst (final name tbc), a free AI audit tool+agent for UK small businesses in five trades: cleaning, plumbing, electricians, landscaping and hair and beauty salons. It's inspired by Restaurant Grader. Like Grader, an owner types their business name, picks it from Google suggestions, and in under a minute gets a letter grade A to F, a score out of 100, and colour-coded findings that come from public data only. What's new is that it's built for these trades, not restaurants. It checks each business against a trusted set of standards for its trade, such as Gas Safe or NICEIC registration, the waste carrier licence and the photos customers expect. The report is visual rather than a wall of text. It shows the business's photo next to its three nearest competitors, and only its top five problems, each with evidence and a before and after. Every problem has a "Fix it for me" button. The agent then does the work itself: it writes review replies, a new profile description, Google posts and a review-request text, and it builds and publishes a simple one-page website. Fixes cost about £5 each or £29 for all of them, plus a re-check after 30 days. I want the scan screen to feel alive, showing what the agent is doing as it works.
